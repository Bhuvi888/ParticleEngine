# 60-Second Demo Video — Shot List

Purpose: gig video on Fiverr, preview video on CodeCanyon, and portfolio clip.
Record the local demo (http://localhost:5173/demo/) with OBS or ScreenToGif.
No voiceover needed — captions convert better and avoid microphone quality risk.

## Settings

- Resolution: 1920×1080 (or 1440×900), 60 fps, no audio track
- Hide desktop icons, dock, notifications; use a clean browser window
- Move the mouse slowly and deliberately; park it out of the way between actions
- Keep each clip 2–4 seconds; cut dead frames

## Alternate opening (ink demo)

If the gig leads with the Japanese ink style, open on
`http://localhost:5173/demo/light/` instead: yin-yang on washi paper, then type
"ZEN" and cycle the 墨 / 朱 / 藍 palette. Everything after 0:16 stays the same.

## Detail shots worth capturing

1. Cursor pushing particles apart (slow figure-8 over the sphere).
2. The exact moment text finishes assembling (start typing ~1s before).
3. The palette transition mid-blend — record 1s before and after the click.
4. Reduced-motion is hard to show on video; skip it, mention it in the description.

## Post-production

- Add captions in the top-left or bottom-center; white text, 24–28 px,
  with a subtle shadow; keep each caption under 5 words.
- Add a 0.5s crossfade between sections, no zoom or camera shake.
- Export H.264 MP4, under 50 MB (Fiverr limit is 50 MB; aim for 10–20 MB).
- Suggested filename: `particle-morph-demo.mp4`.

## Fiverr image assets

Fiverr gig images: 1280×769 (4:3) recommended, up to 3 images.

1. `demo/preview.png` cropped to 1280×769 — sphere hero shot, add text
   "Morphing particle animation".
2. `demo/preview-text.png` cropped — the "HELLO WORLD" formation with caption
   "Text & logo formations".
3. `demo/react-preview.png` cropped — embedded section with caption
   "Works with React & HTML".

If you're targeting ink, wellness, or Japanese-minimal brands, swap #1 for
`demo/light-preview.png` (yin-yang on washi paper) and #2 for
`demo/light-text.png` ("ZEN"). Fiverr only shows three images per gig, so
choose the set that matches the buyers you want.
